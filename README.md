# Austin Permit Explorer

*Disclaimer: This is a personal project and not affiliated with the City of Austin. No guarantees are made about the accuracy of the data displayed in the app.*

The [Austin Permit Explorer](https://michaelsantulli-permit-explorer.share.connect.posit.cloud) is a web app built with R Shiny to view active building permits in the city of Austin, Texas. App data is queried from the [*Issued Building Permits*](https://data.austintexas.gov/Building-and-Development/Issued-Building-Permits/quv8-5ckq/about_data) dataset on the city's Open Data Portal.

[![](Austin%20Permit%20Explorer.png)](https://michaelsantulli-permit-explorer.share.connect.posit.cloud)

## Using the application:

The app displays currently active residential building permits in the city of Austin on a map along with a summary of total housing units and breakdown of housing type.

You can use the drawing tools in the upper left-hand corner of the app to select a particular area of interest. For example, the custom polygon tool allows you to focus on a particular neighborhood, and the circle tool lets you focus on all permits within a certain radius of a point of interest. After selecting your area, click the **Analyze Selected Area** button to filter the data shown on the right side of the app.

You can also see data about individual permits. Hovering over a point shows the number of associated housing units, and clicking on the point brings up additional information such as the permit ID number.

By right-clicking on the map you can pan the view in three dimensions, useful for centering your custom view alongside the filtered summary statistics. If you hide the About bar on the right side of the app, you can print the page as a PDF or image to share your view.
